How Voilà uses your Google account

Voilà asks for one permission: access to your Google Tasks. It uses it only to show, create, update, complete, reorder and delete your tasks when you ask it to, and to save the time you track in each task's notes.

Voilà runs entirely on your Mac. There is no Voilà server: your tasks travel only between your Mac and Google, and nothing is collected, sold or shared. Read the full Privacy Policy.
